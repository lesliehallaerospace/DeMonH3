Calendar

Unless otherwise noted in the trail notes, hash time is 6:30 pm, hares away at 6:45 pm, and pack away at 7:00 pm sharp. Trail is expected to be completed by 9:00 pm and circle completed no later than 9:15 pm. :contentReference[oaicite:1]{index=1}

Hareline

Hareline for DeMon H3 is composed of volunteers and voluntold FRBs. Please contact the Champs if you’d like to lay a trail. Otherwise, the FRB (first runner to the first beer check) will be assigned to the Hareline during circle. :contentReference[oaicite:2]{index=2}

Hash Cash

Hash cash is $5, covering the finest cheapest beer we can find and minimal snacks. Please contact the Champs if you have a specific allergen or need. Hash cash is accepted via Venmo or cash day-of. :contentReference[oaicite:3]{index=3}

Good to Know

While this kennel is running-focused, trails are laid in an attempt to keep the pack together throughout the run. DeMon H3 welcomes individuals of all backgrounds and fitness levels. Trails are typically 5–7 miles. Bashing (bike hashing) is allowed but you may need to improvise on your own to find trail in non-bikeable areas. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}

Our kennel is committed to creating a respectful and inclusive environment. Some songs may not be sung during our circles. If anyone indicates that a song is inappropriate, it will be stopped and another song picked. We want a safe, welcoming atmosphere for all hashers. :contentReference[oaicite:5]{index=5}

DeMon H3 Marks

Unless otherwise noted in live trail talk, DeMon H3 uses the following marks: :contentReference[oaicite:9]{index=9}

Standard Trail Mark

Typically a blob of flour, but could be chalk/drywall, arrow, or even toilet paper. A minimum of three (3) marks is a true trail. If you no longer see any marks, you are either lost or on a false trail — go back to the last known mark. :contentReference[oaicite:10]{index=10}

No Marks

You are lost or not on trail. Do not blow your whistle or call On-On. Listen for the pack and try to find trail marks. :contentReference[oaicite:11]{index=11}

Danger Can

Faster version of a beer check / FRB check. Beer is hidden nearby. Pass it around; the person who gets it to leak cracks it and drinks/passes. Everyone waits until it’s done to regroup. :contentReference[oaicite:12]{index=12}

Beer Near

This is why we run. Beverages should be nearby, sometimes partially hidden from muggles. :contentReference[oaicite:13]{index=13}
