DeMon H3 is a Monday night runner’s kennel in Detroit
with just enough beer checks to have a good time,
while still getting you home early enough for school in the morning.

Guiding Principles

  • To promote physical fitness among our members

  • To promote camaraderie

  • To get rid of weekend hangovers

  • To acquire a good thirst and to satisfy it in beer (or your beverage of choice)

  • To persuade the older members that they are not as old as they feel

DeMon H3 Marks

Unless otherwise noted in live trail talk, DeMon H3 uses the following marks:

Standard Trail Mark

Typically a blob of flour, but could be a chalk or drywall mark, arrow, or even a piece of toilet paper. A minimum of three (3) marks is required for a true trail. If you no longer see any marks, you are either lost or on a false trail. Return to the last known mark or check.

No Marks

You are lost or not on trail. Do not blow your whistle or call On-On. Listen for the pack and try to find trail marks.

Check

This indicates trail may go in any of 369 degrees. When you see this you should call 'check'. When checking for a trail and you see marks do not call On-On until you have seen (3) marks. Call 'On 1' and 'On 2' until you see the third mark to indicate true trail. If you continue on and see a 'F' or BC #' then you have fallen victim to a devious hare’s false trail. Return to the last check and try a different direction. Indicate that this particular section of trail is false by adding a pack comment to the check before checking another trail direction. Checks help keep the pack together, and help slow down the pack. If the check has a blob of flour in the center, it can be used to ‘kick out’ the trail in the direction the pack headed in lieu of a pack comment.

Trail Arrow

The same as a trail mark. Hares leave these to indicate direction changes, street crossings, turns, etc. They still count as marks and falses still could be encountered. Arrows never lie (but hares do....).

Pack Arrow

Used by the pack to indicate which direction they have checked. These are not hare marks but might help you find where everyone in front of you went. Typically in a different color chalk than the hares are using.

True Trail

You’re on true trail and not a false (unless the hare has messed up). Often used after a confusing section of trail to ease the minds of the pack.

False or YBF
(You've Been Fucked)

Even if you have seen (3) marks you may still encounter a false. Return to the last check and try a different trail direction.

Back Check (any number)

Similar to a check, but requires the pack to demonstrate basic counting skills. Head backwards along trail, counting back a number of marks indicated by the BC. That mark then becomes a check. Hares use this to force the pack to run in the wrong direction. For example if you see 'BC 4' it means count backwards (4) marks. The 4th mark will become a check.

Turkey Eagle Split

Trail goes in two different directions. Turkey trail is usually shorter, Eagle trail longer.
They will eventually meet up on the same trail again.

Whichy Way

Trail could go in either of two (or more directions); the first mark seen after the whichy way is true trail.

Danger Can

A faster version of a beer check or FRB check. If you see DC, then there will be a beer hidden in the nearby vicinity. Once the beer is found, pass it around the hounds gathered, taking turns to smash it on your forehead. The person who gets it to spring a leak first cracks it open and either drinks the entirety or passes it around the circle until it’s finished. Every hound that comes to this mark should stop until the DC is consumed and the entire pack is away again. This is used to slow down and regroup the pack.

Beer Near

And there is much rejoicing ... this is why we run. Beverages should be nearby, but may be partially hidden to avoid discovery by muggles.

On-In

The end of the hash.

Calendar

Unless otherwise noted in the trail notes, hash time is 6:30 pm, hares away at 6:45 pm, and pack away at 7:00 pm sharp. Trail is expected to be completed by 9:00 pm and circle completed no later than 9:15 pm.

Hareline

Hareline for DeMon H3 is composed of volunteers and voluntold FRBs. Please contact the Champs if you’d like to lay a trail. Otherwise, the FRB (first runner to the first beer check) will be assigned to the Hareline during circle.

Hash Cash

Hash cash is $5, covering the finest cheapest beer we can find and minimal snacks. Please contact the Champs if you have a specific allergen or need. Hash cash is accepted via VenMo or cash day-of.

Good to Know

While this kennel is running-focused, trails are laid in an attempt to keep the pack together throughout the run. DeMon H3 welcomes individuals of all backgrounds and fitness levels. Trails are typically 5-7 miles.
Bashing (bike hashing) is allowed but you may need to improvise on your own to find trail in non-bikeable areas. Our kennel is committed to creating a respectful and inclusive environment. As such, some songs may not be sung during our circles. Please respect our community values, and if anyone in the kennel indicates that a song is inappropriate, then it will be stopped, no questions asked, and another song will be picked. Our goal is to maintain a safe and welcoming atmosphere for all hashers.

Attendance & Mismanagement

Our mismanagement is not determined by free and fair elections, but rather by a meritocracy of the top six attendees over the trailing twelve months. This approach allows for a seamless continuity of leadership based on active participation.
